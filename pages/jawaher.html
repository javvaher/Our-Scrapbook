About me

A little more about me.

I’m Jawaher, an IT Multimedia student at the University of Sharjah. I’m drawn to creative work, small details, and anything that feels visually organized.

I like combining technology with design, especially when I can experiment with color, layout, and storytelling. I’m also the type of person who notices when something is slightly off, even if nobody else does.
