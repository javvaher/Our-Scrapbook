About me

A little more about me.

I’m Hala, a Digital Media Design student at the University of Sharjah. I am a creative and ambitious person who enjoys transforming ideas into meaningful visual experiences.

I have a strong interest in graphic design, digital content creation, photography, and multimedia production. I believe that great design is not only about appearance, but also about communication, creativity, and delivering a clear message.

Through my university projects and personal work, I keep exploring new ideas and challenging myself to grow as a designer.
